# Revenue Conversion Target v0.1

Status: OPEN / TARGET

## Objective

Generate enough verified cash to fund a Los Angeles trip.

The project does not assume a fixed trip budget yet. The first financial milestone is the
first externally verified payment; later milestones scale toward the trip target.

## Operating thesis

Do not begin by building a marketplace or a broad SaaS product.

Use the model-plus-tool capability substrate to sell an executable business outcome:
a concrete workflow implemented, repaired, automated, verified, or integrated.

Current market signal:
- Upwork's June 2026 hiring report says AI Apps & Integration was its strongest overall
  growth category in June, +34% month over month and +33% year over year.
- Upwork currently exposes a large set of AI automation specialist jobs, including cold-email
  setup, lead-generation automation, and GHL/n8n implementation work.
- 2026 marketplace research describes rising value for AI-augmented professional work that
  connects AI tools to real workflows and business outcomes.

These are market signals, not guarantees of acquisition or earnings.

## First commercial wedge

Productized result, not generic "AI consulting":

**Workflow Conversion Sprint**
- Input: one repetitive, revenue-linked or operationally expensive workflow.
- Output: working implementation, verification receipt, handoff notes, and measurable before/after
  evidence where available.
- Scope: one workflow at a time.
- Commercial unit: fixed-price outcome rather than open-ended hourly labor.

Candidate workflow families:
- lead intake -> qualification -> response;
- document intake -> extraction -> structured output;
- CRM/admin synchronization;
- appointment/request routing;
- reporting/brief generation;
- QA/data cleanup;
- repository/tooling automation.

## Revenue ladder

Use the first sale to establish the mechanism.

R0: paid diagnostic / narrowly-scoped repair.
R1: fixed-price workflow implementation.
R2: multi-workflow package.
R3: recurring monitoring/maintenance.
R4: reusable product/API once repeated demand proves which primitive recurs.

Do not skip directly to R4.

## Conversion equation

A commercial attempt is:

```
Prospect
  -> Pain/constraint observation
  -> Concrete outcome specification
  -> Proposal
  -> Acceptance
  -> Execution
  -> Verification
  -> Payment receipt
  -> Reusable primitive
```

The critical output is **cash + evidence**, not the proposal itself.

## Kill conditions

Kill or repackage an offer when repeated attempts show:
- no paid acceptance;
- high delivery effort relative to price;
- outcome cannot be externally verified;
- dependency on unavailable customer access;
- acquisition cost/friction dominates delivery economics.

Do not interpret conversation enthusiasm, impressions, clicks, or generated proposals as revenue.

## First experiment

Construct a small prospect set around one narrowly defined workflow problem.
Run a fixed outreach/offer experiment.
Track:

- prospects contacted;
- qualified problems;
- proposals;
- accepted proposals;
- paid conversions;
- cash collected;
- delivery hours;
- tool/API cost;
- gross margin;
- time from first contact to payment;
- verification completeness.

The first meaningful success criterion is:

**cash collected from an externally verified customer transaction.**

## Strategic constraint

The system may use AI heavily, but must sell an outcome that the customer values independently
of the use of AI.

No claim of generalized autonomous agency is required.
